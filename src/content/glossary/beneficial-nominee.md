---
term: "Beneficial Nominee"
category: "Legal & Claims"
plainEnglishSummary: "A nominee who is the spouse, parent, or child of the policyholder. Under Indian law, they receive absolute title and ownership over the insurance death claim proceeds, overriding other legal heirs."
legalOrRegulatoryBasis: "Section 39(7) of the Insurance Act, 1938."
commonTrapOrConfusion: "Distant relatives, siblings, or friends are not Beneficial Nominees. If a sibling is named, they are merely a collector/receiver under Indian succession law and must distribute funds among legal heirs."
relatedTerms: ["Nomination", "Legal Heir", "Section 39"]
---

Under the landmark 2015 amendment to Section 39, the concept of a Beneficial Nominee resolved decades of Indian family litigation by giving immediate family members conclusive ownership of life insurance death benefits without requiring succession certificates or probate.
