---
term: "Sum Assured"
hindiTransliteration: "Bima Rashi"
category: "Policy Fundamentals"
plainEnglishSummary: "The guaranteed minimum capital sum promised by the insurance company to be paid to beneficiaries upon the death of the life assured, or upon maturity in guaranteed policies."
legalOrRegulatoryBasis: "Section 2(11) of the Insurance Act, 1938 and IRDAI Product Regulations."
commonTrapOrConfusion: "Do not confuse Sum Assured with Sum Assured on Death. Sum Assured on Death is often defined as higher of 10x annualized premium or 105% of all premiums paid."
relatedTerms: ["Sum Assured on Death", "Premium", "Beneficiary"]
---

The Sum Assured represents the baseline financial shield of an insurance policy. In pure term insurance, it is the exact payout delivered to nominees. In investment-linked or endowment plans, the death benefit may be higher or lower depending on market performance or accrued bonuses.
