---
term: "Free-Look Period"
category: "Consumer Rights"
plainEnglishSummary: "A statutory 30-day window from the date of receiving the policy bond during which a policyholder can review the terms, cancel the contract without penalty, and obtain a full premium refund (less minor administrative deductions)."
legalOrRegulatoryBasis: "IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests and Operations of Insurers) Regulations, 2024."
commonTrapOrConfusion: "The 30-day clock begins on the day you RECEIVE the physical or electronic policy document, NOT on the day you paid the premium or submitted the proposal form."
relatedTerms: ["Policy Bond", "Mis-selling", "Cancellation"]
---

Under IRDAI's landmark 2024 regulations, the free-look period was standardized to 30 days across all distribution channels (online, agents, and bancassurance). Permitted deductions upon cancellation are strictly restricted to pro-rata risk premium for the period on risk, medical examination expenses incurred by the insurer, and stamp duty charges.
