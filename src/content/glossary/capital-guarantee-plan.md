A Capital Guarantee Solution is not a distinct statutory insurance product approved by the IRDAI, but a packaged bundling of two separate insurance policies. Typically, 55% to 60% of the customer's annual premium is deposited into a traditional non-participating endowment plan so that its maturity payout contractually matches 100% of all premiums deposited across both policies. The remaining 40% to 45% is invested into a market-linked ULIP fund. While principal loss is prevented at full maturity, the heavy allocation to low-yielding debt dampens equity growth.
