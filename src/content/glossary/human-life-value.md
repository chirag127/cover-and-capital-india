Human Life Value (HLV) is the financial and actuarial benchmark for quantifying an individual's economic worth to their dependants. Mathematically formulated by Solomon S. Huebner, HLV computes the present value of future earnings minus the individual's personal living expenses, discounted at the risk-free rate. Insurers use age-based HLV income multipliers (typically 20x to 30x annual income for ages 20–35, decreasing to 10x–15x for ages 45–55) to cap the maximum permissible life insurance sum assured.
