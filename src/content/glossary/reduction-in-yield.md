---
term: "Reduction in Yield (RIY)"
category: "Actuarial & Cost Analysis"
plainEnglishSummary: "The difference between the gross rate of return generated by an insurer's underlying investment fund and the net rate of return actually credited to the policyholder after all internal charges, mortality costs, and taxes are deducted."
legalOrRegulatoryBasis: "IRDAI (Unit Linked Insurance Products) Regulations, 2019 (Regulation 25)."
commonTrapOrConfusion: "A ULIP fund NAV may grow at 12%, but an investor's cash-flow return might only be 9.8%. The 2.2% difference is the Reduction in Yield caused by ongoing fees and unit cancellation."
relatedTerms: ["Fund Management Charge", "Mortality Charge", "Net Yield"]
---

Reduction in Yield (RIY) measures the cumulative cost drag of an insurance wrapper. Under IRDAI regulations, RIY for ULIPs is capped at 2.25% for policies with terms exceeding 15 years, ensuring that insurers cannot dilute customer returns through excessive backdoor administrative deductions.
