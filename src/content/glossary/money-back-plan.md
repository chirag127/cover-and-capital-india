A Money Back Plan is an Indian insurance contract structured to provide periodic liquidity to policyholders at specified intervals (such as every 4 or 5 years) while maintaining life coverage. Popular examples include LIC Money Back Plan 20 Years (Plan 920) and LIC Dhan Rekha (Plan 863). Because capital is distributed in nominal chunks that are often consumed by households rather than reinvested, the overall purchasing power return of money-back plans is severely impaired by inflation.
