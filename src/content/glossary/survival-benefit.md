Survival Benefits represent pre-scheduled, milestone-based liquidity distributions paid to the life assured upon surviving designated policy years. For example, a 20-year money-back plan may pay 20% of the Basic Sum Assured at the end of the 5th, 10th, and 15th policy years. Under Schedule II(2) of the Income-tax Act, 2025, survival benefits are tax-exempt provided total annual non-ULIP premiums across all policies do not exceed INR 5,00,000.
