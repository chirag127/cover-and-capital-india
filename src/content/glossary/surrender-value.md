---
term: "Surrender Value"
category: "Policy Servicing"
plainEnglishSummary: "The actual cash amount paid by a life insurance company to a policyholder when they voluntarily terminate their policy contract before its scheduled maturity date."
legalOrRegulatoryBasis: "IRDAI Master Circular on Life Insurance Products, 2024 (Clause 14)."
commonTrapOrConfusion: "In early years (years 1 to 3), Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV) can be as low as 30% to 35% of total premiums paid minus first year premium, resulting in massive capital destruction."
relatedTerms: ["Guaranteed Surrender Value", "Special Surrender Value", "Paid-Up Policy"]
---

Surrender values are divided into Guaranteed Surrender Value (GSV) and Special Surrender Value (SSV). Under IRDAI's revised 2024 norms, insurers must provide enhanced SSV scales for policies surrendered after the first year to improve policyholder equity.
