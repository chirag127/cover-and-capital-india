## Detailed Analysis
When evaluating whether to buy a ULIP or unbundle your financial planning, the most important metric is **protection adequacy**. A 30-year-old software engineer earning ₹15 Lakh per year needs at least ₹1.5 Crore to ₹2 Crore of term protection. A ULIP with a ₹1 Lakh annual premium provides only ₹10 Lakh of cover, leaving family dependants severely exposed in the event of an untimely death.
