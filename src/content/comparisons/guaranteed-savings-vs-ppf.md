## Detailed Analysis
For conservative Indian savers seeking safety of principal, the Public Provident Fund (PPF) is the gold standard for sovereign-backed debt accumulation. Unlike life insurance companies which must invest in long-term corporate paper and deduct insurer overheads, the Government of India credits 100% of PPF interest directly to the subscriber with zero expense ratios.
